01 · PROFILE

About

Background, Upbringing & Direction

I was born and brought up in Siliguri, West Bengal, in a family with a long-standing business background, with entrepreneurship and trade being carried forward through generations.

Although I initially had limited interest in business, as I grew older, my curiosity gradually developed towards entrepreneurship, finance, marketing, investing and financial markets.

During my school years, I participated in several entrepreneurial activities, both within school and outside, which strengthened my interest in business and understanding how organisations operate and grow.

After completing school, I wanted an educational path that would allow me to gain business knowledge while retaining the flexibility to explore practical opportunities and personal interests. This led me to discover the BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship programme at IIM Bangalore.
